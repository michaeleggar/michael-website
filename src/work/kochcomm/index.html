Custom post types and filtering

I built custom post types for services, work, team members, and job openings, each with its own templates and custom fields. The work archive includes category-based filtering so users can browse projects by service and industry.

Designer collaboration

I worked closely with the designer throughout the build. The comps left room for interpretation, especially around interaction and responsive behavior, so a large part of my role was making decisions in those gaps and checking back in to keep the implementation aligned.

Continued development

The site has kept evolving since launch. As the team’s front-end developer, I’ve handled ongoing requests including new case studies, SEO work, and a custom timeline page for the company’s 20th anniversary.
