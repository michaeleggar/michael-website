Koch Comm website

A new site for an Oklahoma City marketing and advertising agency, built to better represent the team and showcase its work and services.

Client
Koch Comm
Role
Front-end development
Platform
WordPress, Elementor, PHP, JavaScript, CSS
Live site
kochcomm.com ↗

I developed the site in close collaboration with the designer, translating the visual direction into a responsive system for the agency’s services, work, team, and ongoing content.

Build and collaboration

I built custom post types for services, work, team members, and job openings, each with its own templates and custom fields. The work archive includes category-based filtering by service and industry.

I worked closely with the designer throughout the build. The comps left room for interpretation around interaction and responsive behavior, so a large part of my role was making decisions in those gaps and checking back in to keep the implementation aligned.

Ongoing work

The site has kept evolving since launch. As the team’s front-end developer, I’ve handled ongoing requests including new case studies, SEO work, and a custom timeline page for the company’s 20th anniversary.
