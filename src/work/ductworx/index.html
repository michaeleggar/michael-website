DuctWorx website

A modular website that carries DuctWorx’s industrial identity while giving its internal team control over everyday updates.

DuctWorx needed a website that matched its new brand identity and allowed a nontechnical team to build and update pages without losing visual consistency. I designed the site and developed a reusable content system around the company’s products, people, and industrial photography.

Challenge

The site needed to feel as direct and capable as the company itself, while remaining flexible enough for people who do not work in code. An entirely open page builder would make updates easy, but could quickly produce inconsistent pages.

The design therefore had to work as both a visual system and a set of useful constraints.

What I built

I created a library of reusable ACF sections, each with a defined content structure and a small set of display options. The team can assemble new pages and reorder content without touching code, while the system preserves typography, spacing, and responsive behavior.

Navigation, product information, team content, and calls to action are all editable through WordPress. The result is a site the client can operate independently without turning every update into a development request.
