In the scene, a teenager with cancer is up at night in a group chat with friends who understand. I built the app in Electron as two windows sharing one conversation: the chat the camera sees, where the actor types and sends, and a crew window for adding characters, setting who’s online, sending as anyone on cue, and switching between messages and video chat. After the first shoot day I added saved lines with an adjustable typing delay, in-place message editing, and a reset between takes.

The original grew fast under production pressure, so after the shoot I rebuilt it with React and Vite. One reducer drives both windows and keeps them in sync, and the rebuild adds chat rooms, a settings panel that pops out into its own window, and a layout that works on a phone. Try either version below: cue a line and the character types for a moment before it lands.
