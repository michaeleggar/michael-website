The homepage sorts its audience with tags, so it reads as a map before the actual map appears. Innovation Hall, the district’s coworking and event space, gets a tab for each room, each ending with the thing someone came to do. Events and news are post types the OKCID team publishes directly; they reach the homepage in order and move to the archive once they’ve passed.

The Map & Directory page plots every organization in the district on a live map that flies between its landmarks, with a directory beneath it that filters by field. Innovation Hall has a page of its own, with its hours up front and a tab for each space someone can rent.
