The homepage asks who you are before it shows you anything. People, Ideas, and Industries each pull up their own tags and photograph, so a resident and a startup founder each find their own way in. The Map & Directory page plots every organization in the district on a live map that flies between its landmarks, with a directory beneath it that filters by field.

Innovation Hall is the district’s coworking and event space, and it gets a page of its own, with the hours up front and a tab for each room that ends at a Book Now button. Events and news are posts the OKCID team publishes themselves. They line up on the homepage by date and move to the archive once they’ve passed.
