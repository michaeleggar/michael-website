The office wanted to keep the site current on its own, so I built every section in WordPress with Advanced Custom Fields, from the product rows to the values table. The team picks one, fills it in, and the layout, type, and spacing come with it. Since launch, they’ve added projects, updated the roster, and rewritten copy without a single developer request.
