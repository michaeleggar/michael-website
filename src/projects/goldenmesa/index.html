I gave the navigation three verbs. Play, Eat, and Stay each open onto that part of the property, with photographs of what’s inside. Promotions were the part that could never wait on a developer. A hot seat drawing pulls winners from whoever’s playing for a few hours on a Monday afternoon, and then it’s over, so promotions and rooms are their own kind of post. The marketing team writes one up the way they’d write a news item, it shows up in the homepage carousel and on the promotions page, and it drops off by itself when it ends.
