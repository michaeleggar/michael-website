The navigation is three verbs, Play, Eat, and Stay, each opening onto the part of the property behind it. Rooms and promotions are their own post types, so the marketing team adds a hot seat drawing the way they’d write a post: it appears on the homepage carousel and the promotions page on its own, and drops off when it ends.
