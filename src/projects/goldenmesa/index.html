Our team stayed at the hotel during discovery. The property was beautiful, filled with warm southwestern details under a wide-open Oklahoma sky. My time there, along with photos I took, became the starting point for the site’s colors, textures, and typography.

On the development side, the big thing to solve was promotions. Those used to be static graphics designed and uploaded as images, so I built a new events and promotions system with ACF and a custom flatpickr calendar. Their team fills in a post with dates, times, and info, and visitors can filter by type or pick a date range to see what’s going on while they’re planning a visit.
