Scott, our creative director, designed the site, and we worked closely while I built it. We planned from the start for me to shape the interactions and responsive behavior during the build, checking back in with him as they came together.

On the development side, services, case studies, team members, testimonials, and open positions are each their own post type, so a new hire or a new client goes up as easily as a blog post. The work archive filters by service and industry, so someone looking for nonprofit branding finds it without wading through everything else. Since launch I’ve kept iterating and building on it as needs come up from the team, including a timeline page for the agency’s 20th anniversary and landing pages for its web audit and crisis communications plans.
