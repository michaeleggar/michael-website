I built the site from the agency’s comps, settling what they left open, the hover states, the breakpoints, how the case study carousel moves. Services, case studies, team members, and job openings are each their own kind of post, so a new hire or a new client goes up the same way a blog post does. The work archive filters by service and by industry, so someone looking for public relations work in healthcare finds it without wading through everything else.
