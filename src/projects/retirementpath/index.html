Their brand is built around the path to retirement, so I let that idea guide the shapes. The curve of the homepage hero nods to a path, and that same curvature carries into the photos and heroes on the interior pages. I introduced a serif typeface to feel confident and steady, and used their gold to pick out key phrases.

On the development side, I focused on making it easy for them to keep publishing new content for their clients. I built a content library where their team adds a financial brief, an e-book, or market commentary, picks where it belongs, and templates place it across the site automatically. They can also mark anything as new, and a small pill shows up on it everywhere it appears, so returning visitors can spot what’s changed.
