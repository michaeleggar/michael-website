A new piece gets a type and a few tags, and that’s the whole job. It lands in the right archive, shows up on the service pages it relates to, and takes its turn on the homepage. Nobody has to place it. The firm’s navy and gold stayed. I gave them room, with a big serif headline, more air between sections, and photographs of people. The rest of the site works the same way, built from sections the team combines on their own.
