The library runs on custom post types with fields and tags in ACF. A new piece gets a type and a few tags, and the site does the rest: it lands in the right archive, shows up in the related lists on service pages, and takes its turn on the homepage. Pages are built from sections the team combines on their own, and I kept the firm’s classic navy and gold, opening the site up around them.
